Cadence Music Import Instructions

1. Copy the "Sample Song" folder and rename the copy to the real song title.
2. Put that song's files in the renamed folder. For the full typing-follow effect, use two synchronized stems from the same Ultimate Vocal Remover run:

   Song Name_(Vocals).wav
   Song Name_(Instrumental).wav

3. Do not trim or time-stretch only one stem. Both files must share the same start point and duration.
4. Cadence refreshes the playlist automatically. You can also choose "Music Resources" in the app to confirm the active directory.

WAV or a high-quality MP3 is recommended. M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WEBM, MID, and MIDI are also recognized.
A single audio file can play normally, but the complete reveal effect requires both Vocal and Instrumental stems.
Process and play only music you own or are authorized to use. This text file is not added to the playlist.
